Learning Marginal Viewpoint Utility for Budgeted Evidence Selection in RAG

Yiyang Duan, Ziqi Yu, Tiantong Wu, Yebo Wu, Wei Yang Bryan Lim
3 minread4.30score

Multi-viewpoint RAG must keep distinct answers/stances under a fixed context budget. LMVU ranks by the marginal viewpoint coverage a candidate adds to the current selection, trained with within-state comparisons on development viewpoint labels, and rescoring budget-feasible candidates as context grows. It reaches 94.6% coverage on Perspectrum and matches/nears full coverage on WVS/ESS10, beating strong baselines. Rank multi-viewpoint evidence by marginal coverage, not per-doc relevance alone.

1. Problem

Relevance-greedy selection re-covers one viewpoint until the budget dies. Need explicit marginal coverage utility.

2. Method

Train on within-state marginal coverage gains; at inference, greedy Algorithm 1 fills budget B. Optional MMR-oversampled pools (e.g., 4×) then refine.

3. Core claim

Diversity becomes marginal coverage of annotated viewpoint units. Relevant evidence that adds no coverage should yield.

4. Experiments

94.6% on Perspectrum; WVS ΔRVC vs MMR often excludes 0; dropping state features collapses WVS NDCG@5 ~0.66→0.44.

5. Limits

Needs viewpoint labels; budget is tokenizer-dependent; unlabeled domains need proxies.

6. Practice

For contested QA RAG, train within-state marginal coverage objectives instead of similarity-only selection.
