Ranking Bandits for Carousel Interfaces with Observable Browsing Depth

Takuma Yasuda, Atsuyoshi Nakamura, Dai Hai Nguyen
6 minread4.00score

In carousels users stop at a browsing depth; slots below that depth are neither clicked nor exposed, yet classical PBM still treats them as seen-but-not-clicked. Under observable depth $V(t)$, this paper proposes OD-UCB / OD-TS / OD-DMED-L that update stats only from truly exposed positions. OD-UCB has an instance-dependent log regret bound; OD-DMED-L’s asymptotic upper bound matches the lower-bound leading term. At T=500,000 on shallow/deep synthetic and RecGaze-calibrated environments, OD-TS matches informed PBM-TS on final mean regret (1121.8 vs 1159.9 shallow) while all OD methods beat PBM-UCB. When browse depth is observed, do not treat unseen slots as negative feedback.

1. Problem

Each round shows an L-slot ranking; the user stops after depth $V(t)$; clicks occur only on observed slots with unknown attractiveness θi​. PBM folds unobserved slots into the likelihood via examination probabilities Pj​. Here $V(t)$ is observed and independent of display order; the goal is expected-click maximization / regret minimization.

2. Method

Exposure and click counts ni​(t),si​(t) accumulate only for positions j≤V(s). OD-UCB ranks by UCB of observed means; OD-TS samples Beta posteriors; OD-DMED-L follows DMED-style exploration. Baselines PBM-UCB/TS still get true Pj​ but not realized depth.

3. Core claim

Observable depth replaces a position-bias table: OD-UCB’s leading term avoids a PL−2​ factor vs PBM-UCB; OD-DMED-L is asymptotically tight; OD-TS needs neither true Pj​ nor non-conjugate rejection sampling.

4. Experiments

$K=50$, $L=5$, T=5×105; shallow/deep synthetic and RecGaze-driven (P and CTR from eye-tracking carousels). Final mean regrets:

5. Limits

Independent clicks and ranking-independent depth are assumptions; RecGaze is log-calibrated simulation, not an online deployment.

6. Practice

If real browse depth is logged, mask unexposed slots in bandit updates. Existing PBM-TS stacks can A/B OD-TS to drop dependence on Pj​ while keeping similar regret.
