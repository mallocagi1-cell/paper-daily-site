1. Optimal Fusion Weights Form Intervals, Not Points: A Latency-Aware Study of Query-Adaptive WRRF

UC IrvineThomson ReutersUniversity of Guelph

Adaptive fusion need not predict one exact weight. Across 128 configurations, 74 significantly outperform standard RRF after multiple-testing correction.

Key insight: Treat optimal weights as sets rather than arbitrary point targets, and include predictor latency in deployment decisions.

2. Preference Is Not Position: Evaluating Reranking on NevIR in Fixed Hybrid Candidate Pools

Correct pairwise preference does not guarantee a useful list position. Bounded Qwen3 reranking reaches preferred-passage MRR of 0.7509 in fixed hybrid pools.

Key insight: Evaluate pair preference and full-list position separately; optimizing one can degrade the other.

3. Cross-Pollination Retrieval: Finding Transferable Ideas Across Fields in Scientific Literature

KAISTUniversity of TübingenNAVER

Cross-topic citations teach retrieval models to find transferable ideas. Contrastive training raises Recall@10 from the best untrained retriever’s 0.26 to as high as 0.70.

Key insight: Training labels define relevance. Topic similarity alone can miss useful analogies and may impair cross-domain retrieval.

4. Beyond Recall Saturation: Scaling Hard-Negative Reranker Training for Industrial Legal Retrieval

Deli Joint LaboratoryCity University of Macau

A two-stage hard-negative pipeline improves legal reranking after candidate recall saturates. The trained model wins 72.4% of non-tied internal offline preference comparisons.

Key insight: Separate candidate recall from reranker quality. More dynamic mining stages do not consistently improve held-out performance.

5. Conditional information Retrieval for Longitudinal Survey Harmonisation

University of SurreyUCL

Concept-conditioned retrieval separates wording overlap from shared survey constructs. MAP and recall improve by roughly 2–3 points over baselines.

Key insight: Explicit concepts can disambiguate semantic matching, and a general domain thesaurus can provide supervision when task labels are scarce.

6. MemRevise: A Self-Revising Memory for Conversational Search

University of Innsbruck

A revisable topic memory tracks additions, abandonment and reactivation. With a frozen retriever, MemRevise reaches mean MRR@10 of 0.701.

Key insight: Keep topic state transitions explicit so abandoned constraints stop affecting search while remaining recoverable.

7. StrucChunk: Structure-Aware Retrieval for Legal Document RAG

Independent Researcher

Structure-aware legal chunking preserves clause boundaries and resolves cross-references before hybrid retrieval. LegalBench-RAG character Recall@8 reaches 48.18%.

Key insight: Chunk boundaries determine whether evidence is representable at all; preserve document structure before tuning retrieval models.

8. MEMORA: A Memory-Enhanced Multimodal Committee Reranking Agent for Reasoning-Intensive Retrieval

Chungbuk National UniversityUniversity of Innsbruck

MEMORA decomposes queries into factors, gathers evidence and combines committee verdicts with episodic memory. Text-to-text nDCG@10 reaches 41.5 on MM-BRIGHT.

Key insight: A factor-level evidence matrix makes reranking decisions inspectable, but committee calls and cross-query memory add serving complexity.

9. Exact Risk Certificates for Pairwise-Judged Reranking

McGillOhio UniversityUniversity of Manitoba

Exact certificates bound worst-case nDCG loss using pairwise judgments, saving 2.34 comparisons per development query on shared trajectories.

Key insight: Judgments constrain the ideal-ranking denominator as well as the loss numerator; ignoring that coupling wastes comparisons.

10. KhyontekAI at CheckThat! 2026: Multilingual Dense Retrieval and Cross-Encoder Reranking for Scientific Web Claims

Tezpur UniversityKhyontekAI

A metadata-enriched multilingual retriever followed by cross-encoder reranking links scientific social posts to source papers. Average MRR@5 is 0.5862.

Key insight: Audit metadata, candidate depth and language balance separately to identify which parts improve source retrieval.
