📖 Deep dive →

Re3Rank changes what looks at the candidates in attention-based reranking: one model first reads the question and candidates and writes a reasoning trace, then a second model looks with the trace's tokens and ranks candidates by the attention they receive.

2. AttentionIndex: Accelerating Learned Sparse Attention with a Causal Inverted-File Index

📖 Deep dive →

In learned sparse attention, as used by DeepSeek-V4 and GLM-5.3, each query attends to only k keys, but the small scoring network that picks those k keys still scores every key in the prefix. At one million tokens, that step takes more than half of prefill time.

4.36

2. Pixel: A Co-Designed Full-Stack Robot System for Learning General-Purpose Mobile Manipulation

In mobile-manipulation robots, the hardware, the data-collection devices, whole-body control and policy learning are usually built separately, and when they are put together they often hold each other back. This CMU paper designs all four together: a wheeled humanoid that costs under $15,000, with an upper body proportioned like a human, plus a low-cost wearable rig that lets a person record demonstrations without the robot present. Because the body proportions match, human demonstrations can teach the robot directly, and demonstrations recorded with the wearable rig alone are enough to train mobile-manipulation policies. Scored by how far each task gets completed, the full robot gets a perfect score on three ablation tasks, and disabling the torso drops the average to 47.4%, which shows the policy really uses those joints. Teams that want to build a full platform cheaply can take it as a ready starting point.
